## Do
- Home chip (slot from T301a): states idle/synced-at, syncing, offline, server unreachable, pending count, 'N need attention', 'update required', 'fix your device clock, then sync'; tap opens the Sync screen.
- Sync screen: 'Sync now', last result, pending/rejected counts, diagnostics (refused-cache cards with ids, upgrade-required op seq/id/first error, copy to clipboard).
- Rejected ops screen: list per op (what, when, why in plain English per rejection code), per-op Retry/Discard, and 'Re-mint' flow: shows `RemintPreview` count and the full scrollable discard list (grouped under window parent) BEFORE the confirm button is enabled; confirm passes the preview handle to T303d. Unavailable states show the reason.
- All strings via i18n. Previews/Compose tests for state rendering; no network.
