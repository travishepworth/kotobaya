  not undone, order `(reviewed_at, hlc, id)`), train with the py-fsrs 6.3.2 optimizer; minimum review count.
- Held-out split (time-based, by card): compute log-loss of current weights vs new weights on the held-out set;

  via `OpApply` (validated against the schema), then writes `JobResultFsrsOptimize`. Worker never writes
  `app.fsrs_params` (CLAUDE.md rule 8).
- Weekly CronJob `cron-optimize` enqueues one `fsrs-optimize` job per live preset (dedupe `fsrs-optimize:<id>`).
