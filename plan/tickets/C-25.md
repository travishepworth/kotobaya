## User request (2026-11-15)
Review screen, web and Android: (1) kanji card: show the top 3 words using the kanji below the readings, the "smartest" pick, but try to include at least 1 kun'yomi word and 1 on'yomi word, by frequency; (2) vocab card: show the top 3 decks the word is in, by deck priority (draw order).

## Decision (ADR-029, provisional, see DECISIONS)
Kanji example pick (core, pure, jvm+js): input = the kanji's on/kun readings + candidate words (headword, reading, freq rank (null = unranked), inUserDecks/hasNote/word status). Classify each candidate's reading of this kanji as ON / KUN / OTHER by aligning the word reading to its kanji (match the kanji's segment against the kanji's on readings (katakana→hiragana, rendaku/gemination variants) or kun readings (stem before the '.', okurigana)). Score: words the user knows or is learning or has in decks first, then global freq rank ascending, then seq. Pick: best ON + best KUN (if any), then fill to 3 by score; ordered by score. Golden tests with real kanji (e.g. 食: 食べる(KUN), 食事(ON), 食堂/食品…; 生: 生きる, 生活, 先生).
