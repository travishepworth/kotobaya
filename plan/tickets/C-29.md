1. Identity: word audio keyed deterministically (e.g. sha of (engine, voice, normalized reading text)) so server, web and android agree; how a rendered card learns its audio (RenderInput/QueueItem field `word_audio` hash or URL; note `audio` field wins).
2. Generation: server-side — after materialization / for cards due within the prefetch window (SPEC 334, setting name), plus on-demand for a card whose audio is missing (sync GET that returns/creates, or enqueue + client retry). Spell out the endpoint(s) in openapi; reuse POST /tts / tts job where possible.
3. Offline: how Android learns which blobs to prefetch (queue item field or a manifest endpoint) and plays from local cache.
4. Render: template change — speaker button inside/next to `.kb-expression` on the answer side; listening cards on the front. Setting `review.autoplay_audio` (bool, default true) in SCHEMA settings; autoplay on reveal; replay by tap or `P`.
5. HANDOFF: the implementation split into tickets by module (core, server, worker, web, android) with deps.
