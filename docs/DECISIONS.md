- ADR-037 (human, 2026-11-17): One review loop, decided in core — supersedes ADR-031's keep-going scope and `more_available` as UI signal, ADR-033's counts-with-later-learning and single waiting state, ADR-034's "new limits unchanged"; replaces the interleave with a proportional mix that clears reviews and the new goal together (both modes) and removes the session new cap. New core `ReviewLoop.evaluate(QueueInput)` returns the queue to serve plus `LoopState`: phase `reviewing | goal_reached | done_for_today | waiting | empty`, ready now (learning whose step elapsed + reviews due today within the review budget), later today (count + next due, hint only), backlog (all due reviews, and how many beyond the cap), reviews today/limit, new today/goal. Order is always due learning first, then ready reviews (oldest first) and new cards in the proportional mix (amended by the user 2026-11-17: reviews-then-new was a regression). Limits off-toggle (`reviews_unlimited` false): reviews up to the cap mixed with new up to `new_per_day`, then `done_for_today` with Keep going, which clears the backlog first (no mix) and then serves new cards without end. Toggle on: reviews never capped, mixed with new up to the goal; at the new goal `goal_reached` with Keep going, then new cards without end, a review that comes due slotted in next. A card that came due is always the next card; a learning card is never served early; `waiting` only when nothing is ready and no new card is left anywhere. Keep going stays a per-request flag (no op, no setting) but each client persists it for the study day. API: additive `ReviewQueue.loop`; `counts`, `more_available`, `next_learning_due_at` deprecated with frozen meaning; `scheduler.new_per_session` ignored; shell `LOCAL_API_VERSION` 2. No data, op or SCHEDULER_VERSION change.
